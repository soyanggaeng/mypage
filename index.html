My research centers on benchmarking and evaluating language models. I aim to build evaluations that reveal model capabilities and limitations beyond aggregate scores.

Email Google Scholar GitHub

Research interests

  • Benchmark Design
  • Model Evaluation
  • Language Understanding

I study how benchmark design, evaluation protocols, and scoring methods shape our understanding of language models. My work includes Korean language benchmarks, sentence-level negation, and answer-choice sensitivity.

More broadly, I am interested in the reliable and responsible use of AI in real-world settings.
